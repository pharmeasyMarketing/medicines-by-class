About Anti-diabetic Medicines

Anti-diabetic medicines are used in the management of diabetes as part of a treatment plan decided by a doctor. Different groups within the class act in different ways – some support the body's own insulin response, some change how glucose is handled by the kidneys, and insulins replace or supplement insulin directly.

Most anti-diabetic products sold in India are fixed-dose combinations, so two packs with similar names can contain different molecules at different strengths. Always match the pack to the prescription rather than to the brand name you remember.

Sub-classes you will find here

Within this class you will find Combinations, Biguanides, Alpha-glucosidase inhibitors, Sulfonylureas, Thiazolidinediones, DPP-4 inhibitors, Diabetic Neuropathy, Meglitinides, SGLT2 inhibitors, GLP-1 receptor agonists and Insulins. Two medicines from different sub-classes are not alternatives to each other.

Prescription and dosage

Medicines in this class are prescription-only unless the pack states otherwise. Always order the exact strength and dosage form written on your prescription, and continue any monitoring your doctor has advised.

Safety information

  • Keep meals, activity and blood sugar monitoring consistent with your doctor's advice, and tell them about any kidney or liver condition.
  • Do not self-medicate. Use medicines only as advised by a qualified doctor.
  • Check the strength and dosage form on your prescription before ordering.
  • Tell your doctor about allergies and about every other medicine or supplement you take.
  • Do not stop a prescribed medicine without medical advice, even if you feel better.
