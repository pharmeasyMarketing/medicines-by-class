About Antibiotics

Antibiotics are used for infections caused by bacteria. Different groups target different organisms, which is why the choice, dose and duration depend on the site and type of infection rather than on how unwell a person feels.

Completing the prescribed course matters even after symptoms settle. Stopping early, or keeping leftover antibiotics for a future illness, contributes to antibiotic resistance.

Sub-classes you will find here

Within this class you will find Penicillins, Antiseptic, Combinations, Cephalosporins, Fluoroquinolones, Topical Antibiotic, Macrolides, Otic Antimicrobial, Topical Anti-Microbial and Carbapenems. Two medicines from different sub-classes are not alternatives to each other.

Prescription and dosage

Medicines in this class are prescription-only unless the pack states otherwise. Always order the exact strength and dosage form written on your prescription, and continue any monitoring your doctor has advised.

Safety information

  • Never use antibiotics left over from a previous illness, and never share them. Tell your doctor about any previous reaction to an antibiotic.
  • Do not self-medicate. Use medicines only as advised by a qualified doctor.
  • Check the strength and dosage form on your prescription before ordering.
  • Tell your doctor about allergies and about every other medicine or supplement you take.
  • Do not stop a prescribed medicine without medical advice, even if you feel better.
